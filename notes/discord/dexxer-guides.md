## 1. General Tips

Most important skills / stats

* Arms lore is the most important dexxer skill for almost every build. It gives you attack speed, increases special chance, increases recharge of weapon abilities, and allows you to disarm every 30 seconds for a huge damage bonus (25% at 120 skill.) Keep this at 80 at all costs, higher if possible.
* Alchemy is generally considered to be better than parry. Take the defensive abilities, use the defensive calculator. It is VERY easy to hit the defense cap of 75% with Alchemy and Healer codex alone.
* Accuracy is the most important stat, make sure it is at 100. See below for easy ways of getting to the cap for any build.
* Damage Resistance soft cap is around 50%. I still go for more in my builds via codex points.

Mastery Chain

* The most versatile dexxer links are Damage Dealt By Player and Melee Special Chance/Damage. You can fit them in almost every build.
* Most builds work great with 15/15 links (requires 10 redline) so work toward this set
* 30 gold links = 45 bronze links, 30 silver links = 37.5 bronze links. By 18 links I wasn't buying any bronze links and began replacing silver with gold.

Options for Reaching Accuracy Cap

* Use sword and board for extra accuracy from setup bonus. You can do this for any 2H build.
* Trade your worst disposable skill and Arms Lore for Focus, do not drop below 80 arms lore.
* If you have the links, use a couple of accuracy links instead. I have 2 gold accuracy links that I will never sell.
* Use a high level weapon aspect while leveling on your armor.

Other Things

* Builds are tested at 30 links, T15 aspect, 10+ redline points If lower aspect, see the section on accuracy. If you don't like how it plays, consider something else. Builds are still playable without redline so don't worry about that.
* These are just the builds I play, I have no idea if they are "the best" or not but they work well for me.

## 2. Specialty Codex Loadouts

> [R] Muls (Space Force Sargeant) [[R]], OP — 6/3/26, 7:09 AM

Alchemy Codex

Alchemy kit is where you will get a lot of your survivability + a little bit of damage from Acrid. Building it for explosion damage at 80 alchemy is not worth it.

* Bandolier - 3
* Panacea - 3
* Elixir - 3
* Mixed Results - 3

IF POISONING Add 2 points into Acrid and take 1 point in Mixed Results, 1 point in Freedom

Healing Codex

There is a lot of flexibility here, but this is what I use.

* Combat Medic - 2
* Immunize - 2
* Pressure Points - 3
* Triage - 2
* Self Treatment - 3
* Specialist - 1

If you plan on fighting high level mobs with big diseases or bleeds (most level 4 dungeons) I would suggest dropping combat medic to level 1 and maxing out immunize.

You may also consider dropping combat medic altogether (see damage resistance soft cap) and maxing out self treatment. This will give you an extra point which you could throw into immunize along with the point from specialist, or throw it into overheal level 1.

## 3. Sword and Board Poison Dexxer

> [R] Muls (Space Force Sargeant) [[R]], OP — 6/3/26, 4:44 PM

Stance - Fang
Finisher - Assasin
Ability - Bane

Poison Kit

I value defensive abilities here, so I take Enfeeble instead of Accumulate or Efficient.

Direct Dose is great vs low HP mobs (15k or less) and you can drop Enfeeble for Direct Dose if you want to. For higher HP mobs, consider going with Efficient instead.

* Terminal - 3
* Targeted Venom - 3
* Susceptible - 3
* Enfeeble - 2
* Direct Dose - 2

Terminal, Targeted Venom, and Susceptible are non-negotiable in my opinion. The rest you can mess around with and see what you like.

I use 12 redline for an extra point and drop accumulation for level 2 direct dose. If you don't like the RNG aspect then take level 2 accumulation instead.

If fighting high HP mobs, direct does can be dropped altogether and you can max out efficient or accumulation instead depending on what you like. for low HP mobs Direct Dose is INSANELY strong and you may consider just putting 5 points into it if your kill speed is good enough.

## 4. Air Wrestler

Stance Dragon
Ability Haymaker
Finisher One heals, one kills. Both are good so swap around depending on how many mobs you are fighting. Once you land Zen Strike you become VERY tanky. If you kill fast enough you will have very good uptime on the finisher buffs. Try to carry them to the next mob for efficient killing (use ropes!)

Camping vs Item ID

If you are worried about losing your magic weapons to PKs use camping. If you want to do more damage and/or you are good at escaping from PKs use Item ID.

Survivability

A lot of survivability in this build comes from air aspect. If you are low air aspect then you may want to use parry instead of alchemy.
