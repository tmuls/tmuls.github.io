### Poison Kit
I value defensive abilities here, so I take Enfeeble instead of Accumulate or Efficient.

Direct Dose is great vs low HP mobs (15k or less) and you can drop Enfeeble for Direct Dose if you want to.  For higher HP mobs, consider going with Efficient instead.

* Terminal - 3
* Targeted Venom - 3
* Susceptible - 3
* Enfeeble - 2
* Direct Dose - 2

Terminal, Targeted Venom, and Susceptible are non-negotiable in my opinion.  The rest you can mess around with and see what you like.

I use 12 redline for an extra point and drop accumulation for level 2 direct dose.  If you don't like the RNG aspect then take level 2 accumulation instead.

If fighting high HP mobs, Direct Dose can be dropped altogether and you can max out efficient or accumulation instead depending on what you like.  For low HP mobs Direct Dose is INSANELY strong and you may consider just putting 5 points into it if your kill speed is good enough.
